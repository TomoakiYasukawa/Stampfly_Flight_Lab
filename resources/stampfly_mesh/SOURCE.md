# StampFly mesh

stampfly_v1.stl is copied from the StampFly Urban RoadRunner project in this repository. Its upstream source is M5Fly-kanazawa/stampfly_ecosystem, commit ce2873cbab92964165e9bdffcefcca1a88703c54. The source STL coordinates are millimetres with Z forward, X left, Y up axes; the app scales them to metres and maps them to its body frame before drawing.
